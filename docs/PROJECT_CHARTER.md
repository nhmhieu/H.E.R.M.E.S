# PROJECT CHARTER

## Target User
HCMUS IT & Science undergraduate students.

## Problem Statement
Ambiguous academic regulations + overwhelming project deadlines leading to poor time management and academic probation risks.

## 10 Concrete Use Cases
1. Exam study plan generation based on course credits and exam dates.
2. GPA threshold alert to identify if a student is at risk of academic warning.
3. Academic warning rule check to understand specific university policies.
4. Course prerequisite lookup to plan future semesters.
5. Study schedule generation considering available daily hours.
6. Graduation credit check to track progress toward degree completion.
7. Retrieval of specific articles from the student handbook.
8. Calculation of weighted GPA from a list of grades.
9. Classification of student intent (e.g., routing to scheduling vs. regulation lookup).
10. Explanation of complex academic terminology (e.g., "tín chỉ tích lũy").

## Non-Goals
* Will NOT do homework or write assignment code for students.
* Will NOT directly manipulate the university portal.

## Success Metrics
* Routing accuracy >= 90%
* RAG factual citation precision >= 95%
* Schedule validity 100% conflict-free
